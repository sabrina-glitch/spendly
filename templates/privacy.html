Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses and protects your information

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1.Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and a securely hashed password. When you use Spendly, we store the expenses you record, including amounts, categories, dates and any notes you add.

2.How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to provide and improve Spendly: to sign you in, display your expenses, and generate summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3.Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may delete your expenses or account at any time.

4.Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. We may use trusted service providers, such as hosting or font delivery services, which only receive the minimum technical information needed to operate the service.

5.Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team. We will respond to your request as soon as possible.
